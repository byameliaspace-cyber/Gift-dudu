Chapter Six

Happy Birthday,
Papi Uno

كل سنة وإنت طيب يا حبيبي

Selamat ulang tahun, sayangku.

You're the calm, wise one in every storm — the kind of man my family already adores, because somehow your heart beats on the same wavelength as theirs. You make me laugh when I least expect it, you're effortlessly cool without ever trying, and you show affection so openly that I never once feel like I'm loving you alone. And yes — you spoil me rotten. I'm not even a little sorry about enjoying it.

Semoga Allah selalu menjaga langkahmu, memudahkan setiap rencanamu, dan memberimu umur yang panjang penuh berkah. اللهم بارك له في عمره وارزقه الصحة والسعادة. I pray every year gets softer and kinder for you — I plan on being there for all of them.

All my love, always yours, Amelia.
